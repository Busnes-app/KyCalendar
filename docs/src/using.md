# Everyday tasks

## Personal events

Sign in as an everyday user and select a calendar. Create an event with its date, time and time zone. When changing a recurring event, choose whether the change affects one occurrence or the series. Refresh after a conflict before retrying an edit, so you do not overwrite somebody else's change.

## Group calendars

Administrators manage group calendars and grants. Members receive reader, editor or manager access through their groups. A group calendar survives deletion of a directory group; its grants determine who can still reach it. Local groups are managed here; SCIM-owned groups are managed at their source.

## Device access and recovery

Give each CalDAV device its own app password and revoke it when that device is retired. Administrators configure the recovery key, destinations and schedule. Consult the restore runbook before relying on a backup; restored clients may need a full resync and new app passwords.
